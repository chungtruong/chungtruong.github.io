Privacy Policy — Chill Ball Sort: Color Puzzle

Developer: C.T Studio ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in Vietnam
App: Chill Ball Sort: Color Puzzle (Android package com.ctstudio.chillballsort) ("the App")
Effective date: 2026-10-10
Contact: derekcheung1983@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the App handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The App has no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and do not receive your gameplay data.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising software automatically collects some device data (see section 4).
  • For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and where otherwise required by law), the App asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so you can continue playing: level progress and best results, the current puzzle (including undo history), Daily Puzzle streak, booster/hint counters, ad frequency counters (for example how many ads were shown today, used only to limit ads), settings (sound, music, vibration, colorblind symbols, language, theme), and whether you own "Remove Ads". Google's consent tool also stores your privacy choices on your device.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings. The App does not back it up to the cloud.

3. Information we receive directly

We do not collect personal information through the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Google Play may give us aggregated statistics about the App (such as number of installs, ratings, and crash and performance reports from users who have agreed to share diagnostics with Google). This information is provided by Google under its own policies and does not identify you to us.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), to show full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads (for example to get an extra tube or a hint). There are no banner ads.

According to Google's documentation, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device;
  • Device and advertising identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and app set ID (and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device);
  • Product interactions: for example app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information: for example app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • basic device and network information needed to deliver ads (for example device model, operating system version, language, and network or mobile network type).

Google uses this data for advertising (including ad measurement), analytics and fraud prevention. All data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS).

  • Personalized ads are shown only where you have consented or where permitted by law.
  • If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads; wording varies by device). After deletion, apps receive a string of zeros instead of the ID.

More information:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google ad partners: support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903
  • Manage your Google ad settings: myadcenter.google.com

5. Consent and your privacy choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the App shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform, a consent tool certified under the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework). You can consent, refuse, or choose purposes and partners.
  • Some US states: where required by applicable US state privacy laws, the App may show Google's privacy message, including an option to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information / targeted advertising.
  • Change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the App → Settings → Privacy options. (This item is shown when a privacy choice applies in your region.) Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • On your device: delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization in Android settings.
  • Remove Ads purchase: if you buy "Remove Ads", the App no longer shows full-screen ads and does not request rewarded ads for boosters.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR): your consent for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalized advertising; where consent is not given, Google may rely on legitimate interests for limited purposes such as fraud prevention and serving non-personalized or limited ads, as described in Google's policies. Purchase processing by Google Play is necessary to perform the purchase you request.

6. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Purchases (for example "Remove Ads") are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The App only receives a confirmation of the purchase from Google Play (product, purchase status and a purchase token) to unlock the feature and to restore it on a new installation. This confirmation is used on your device only; we do not receive or store your card or payment details, and we do not send purchase information to any server of ours.

8. Sharing and "sale" of personal information

We do not sell your personal information for money and we do not share it with anyone other than Google as described in sections 4–6, or when required by law. Under some US state laws, allowing an advertising SDK to collect identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered "selling", "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out as described in section 5.

9. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google states that it uses appropriate safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Chill Ball Sort → Storage → Clear data).
  • Data collected by Google: retained according to Google's policies. You can delete or reset your Advertising ID in Android settings and manage your data at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails to us: deleted when no longer needed to answer you, or earlier on request.

Because we do not hold any account or gameplay data on servers, there is no server-side data for us to delete. If you need help with data held by Google, contact us and we will point you to the right tools.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Use Settings → Privacy options in the App for ad consent. For any other request, email derekcheung1983@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

12. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country, for example 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

We keep data collection to a minimum: we run no servers and receive no gameplay data. Data sent by Google's SDKs is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the services it uses change. The new version will be posted at https://chungtruong.github.io/chillballsort/privacy.html with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App's update notes.

Chúng tôi không cố ý thu thập thông tin cá nhân của trẻ em. Nếu bạn là cha mẹ/người giám hộ và cho rằng con bạn đã gửi thông tin cá nhân cho chúng tôi (ví dụ qua email), hãy liên hệ để chúng tôi xóa.

13. Bảo mật

Chúng tôi thu thập tối thiểu: không vận hành máy chủ, không nhận dữ liệu chơi. Dữ liệu do SDK của Google gửi đi được mã hóa khi truyền. Không có phương thức truyền hay lưu trữ nào an toàn tuyệt đối.

14. Thay đổi chính sách

Chúng tôi có thể cập nhật chính sách khi Ứng dụng hoặc dịch vụ đi kèm thay đổi. Bản mới sẽ đăng tại https://chungtruong.github.io/chillballsort/privacy.html kèm ngày hiệu lực mới. Thay đổi quan trọng sẽ được nêu trong ghi chú cập nhật của Ứng dụng.
